# Dear student

- [S1] Light and lenses
- [S2] Sound and waves
- [S3] The shortcut
- [S4] Atoms and radioactivity
- [S5] Galaxies and stars
- [S6] Electricity

Dear student. I know that you are very busy. So here, without a moment's delay, I will explain exactly what you need to pass your examination. In that examination, they will ask you:

## [S1] Light and lenses

Explain the moon phases and solar eclipse. Explain how light reflects from objects and mirrors, and explain how light travels through different mediums. How do lenses in your glasses work? And the lenses in photography? And the lenses in telescopes? How do they work?

## [S2] Sound and waves

Waves — you need to know what sound is and how to define it, what's an amplitude, what's a frequency. What are the units that describe these features? How do they move in space?

## [S3] The shortcut

Of course I understand how incredibly busy you are, so here are all the formulas and all of the definitions. All you have to do is memorize them by heart, and hope that when you read the question, you will recognize immediately how to apply these formulas and definitions, and you will succeed, without fail, in getting the correct answer. But before I give you the formulas, I forgot one thing.

## [S4] Atoms and radioactivity

If, unfortunately, you happen to get questions from a different topic, then you will also need to know what an atom is and what its composition is. How does it change? What properties does it have? What is the atomic number? And what is radioactive radiation?

## [S5] Galaxies and stars

You might also get questions about the universe. What are galaxies and stars? How do stars live out their lives, and where do they get their energy?

## [S6] Electricity

And of course you might get questions about electricity. How do objects obtain static electricity? What is the internal composition that leads to this? Calculate the current and the voltage and the resistance, and how those influence one another. You might also need to correctly identify the direction of the flow, as well as understand common electrical safety symbols. Draw your own schemes and apply Ohm's law when calculating combined resistances. So let me, of course, hand you the formulas and definitions for these questions as well.
