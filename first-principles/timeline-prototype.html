The Log button switches the time axis to log₁₀(2050 − year). The asymptote at 2050 is a Kurzweil-style estimate of when the rate of foundational progress would go to infinity. On the log axis, ancient eras compress together and recent decades spread out, because each newer paradigm shift takes less calendar time than the one before.
