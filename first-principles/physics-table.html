The periodic table of physics

still in its 1700s phase · waiting for its Mendeleev

In 1700, chemistry was a mess. The four classical elements still lingered. Phlogiston was real. The luminiferous aether filled all space. Alchemists chased the philosopher's stone, gases were "airs" of different kinds, and Lavoisier had not yet shown that matter is conserved through a reaction. There were maybe forty things people had isolated and a hundred names for what to do with them, but no organising principle. Most of it was a collection of recipes.

Modern physics is in roughly that phase. We have a small number of confidently understood pieces (the Standard Model, general relativity), a larger number of almost-known candidates (dark matter, dark energy, the inflaton), and a much larger pile of speculative entities, open puzzles, and rejected ideas. The page below is an honest attempt to put it all in one table, sorted by how confident we are. Mendeleev has not turned up yet.

◐ Unexplained — phenomena without an entity
Something is there. We see it. We do not yet have a name for what it is.
dark matterunexplained~5× more than ordinary matter, holds galaxies together. No identification. Not a "candidate" — a hole in the table.
dark energyunexplained~70% of cosmic energy budget. Cosmological constant? Quintessence? Something else? Open.
inflationunexplained mechanismEarly-universe expansion. Needs a field, but which? No detection of the inflaton.
matter > antimatterunexplained asymmetryThe universe is matter-rich. The mechanism that broke the symmetry is unknown.
ν massunexplained mechanismNeutrinos have mass. Dirac? Majorana? Seesaw with a heavy right-handed partner? Unclear.
strong CPunexplained suppressionA CP-violating term is allowed in QCD. It is observed to be near zero. Why?
Λ valueunexplained smallnessThe cosmological constant is small but non-zero. QFT predicts a value 120 orders of magnitude too big.
singularitiesunexplained physicsGR predicts infinities — at the centre of every black hole, and at the Big Bang. Curvature diverges, the theory breaks. Quantum gravity should fix it. We don't have quantum gravity.
○ Promising undiscovered — predicted, still plausible
Particles that would slot into the table cleanly and have good theoretical motivation. Searched. Not yet found.
aaxionProposed 1977 (Peccei-Quinn) to fix strong CP. Light boson. Doubles as dark-matter candidate. Tightening searches at ADMX, IAXO, haloscopes.
GgravitonQuantum of gravity. Required by any unification of GR + QM. Almost certainly exists; direct detection probably impossible.
Mmagnetic monopoleDirac 1931. Would explain why electric charge is quantised. Searched in cosmic rays, ice, monopole-catalysed proton decay. Open.
ν_ssterile neutrinoA neutrino with no weak interaction. Natural extension of the SM. Possible dark-matter component. Hints from short-baseline experiments.
N_Rright-handed νRequired if neutrinos are Dirac. Drives the seesaw mechanism for tiny ν masses. Heavy versions might exist at high scales.
PBHprimordial black holesBHs formed in the early universe. Could account for some or all of dark matter in certain mass windows. Constrained but alive.
φ_infinflaton fieldSomething drove cosmic inflation. Many candidate fields. Distinguished by spectral tilt and tensor-to-scalar ratio (still unmeasured).
◌ Frayed — predicted, but evidence is not appearing
Theories with elegant motivation that have not delivered. Still discussed, increasingly disfavoured.
χ̃neutralinoLightest SUSY particle, the canonical WIMP. LHC has not seen it. Natural mass windows mostly excluded.
q̃, ẽsquarks, sleptonsSUSY partners of matter. LHC pushed bounds past 1–2 TeV with no signal. Fine-tuning getting worse.
g̃gluinoSUSY partner of the gluon. Same story — excluded below ~2 TeV.
SUSYsupersymmetry, generallyThe grand prediction. Was supposed to solve hierarchy + give dark matter + unify couplings. None of it has shown up. Not very promising any more.
WIMPweakly-interacting massive particleSpecifically the GeV–TeV thermal-relic class. Direct detection (XENON, LUX-ZEPLIN, PandaX) keeps lowering limits with no signal.
M_GUTgrand unificationSU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) couplings nearly meet at ~10¹⁶ GeV. They meet better with SUSY. Without it, they miss.
p →proton decayPredicted by most GUTs. Super-Kamiokande pushed τ_p past 10³⁴ years with no event. Many GUT variants ruled out.
+EDextra dimensionsCurled-up (Kaluza-Klein) or large (ADD/RS). Would address hierarchy. None detected at LHC or in gravity tests.
ΠpreonSubstructure inside quarks and leptons. Precision tests give compositeness scale > 10 TeV. Out of fashion since the 1990s.
○ Speculative frameworks — mathematical, not particle-level
Whole structures of physics, not specific particles. Mostly mathematical at present.
∞Dstring theory1D vibrating objects in 10D. All particles as harmonics. Mathematically rich, no test at accessible energies.
D-branebranesHigher-dimensional objects strings can end on. Whole zoo of solutions.
M-theory11D frameworkConjectured unification of the five string theories. Mostly a name for what we don't yet have.
LQGloop quantum gravityQuantum gravity built from quantised spacetime networks (spin foams, Penrose). Background-independent. No accessible-scale predictions.
holographyholographic principleAll information in a volume of space is encoded on its boundary. 't Hooft & Susskind, 1990s. The general claim.
AdS/CFTMaldacena dualityConcrete realisation of holography. Quantum gravity in anti-de Sitter space = a conformal gauge theory on its boundary. Works in toy universes.
ER=EPRwormhole = entanglementEntangled pairs connected by tiny wormholes? Maldacena & Susskind, 2013. Suggestive math, no experimental hook.
dS / AdSde Sitter / anti-de SitterMaximally symmetric spacetimes with positive (dS, our universe) or negative (AdS, theoretical) cosmological constant. Playgrounds for quantum-gravity dualities.
DSecdark sectorA whole hidden world with its own forces and particles. Anything could be in here.
∞UmultiverseOther universes with different constants. Unfalsifiable in principle. Anthropic explanations live here.
? Open puzzles — questions with no entity
Things that bug the field even when nothing is obviously "missing"
why 3 families?Three copies of fermions. One copy is enough for atoms. There are three. Nobody knows why.
why these masses?Mass ratios span 12 orders of magnitude (electron to top). No pattern explains them.
hierarchyHiggs mass should be Planck-scale. It is electroweak-scale. Fine tuning of 1 part in 10³⁴.
cosmological ΛQFT predicts Λ ~120 orders of magnitude too big. Worst prediction in physics.
strong CPA CP-violating θ-term is allowed in QCD. It is observed to be zero. Why?
quantum gravityGR + QM do not combine. No controlled theory at the Planck scale.
measurementQuantum collapse, many-worlds, decoherence, Bohmian — still no consensus.
info paradoxWhat happens to information that falls into a black hole?
arrow of timeMicrophysics is time-symmetric. Macrophysics is not. Why?
what is the vacuum?QFT vacuum is full of structure. We cannot calculate its energy correctly.
naturalnessDimensionless ratios should be O(1). Several are not, and there is no story for that.
inflation startInflation explains a lot. What started it? What set the initial conditions?
μ g−2 anomalyMuon magnetic moment deviates from prediction. New physics or theory error?
why locality?QFT is local. But entanglement, holography, and ER=EPR all hint locality is emergent.
why one universe?Or are there many? The multiverse is unfalsifiable but the parameters look fine-tuned.
classical limitWhen and how does quantum branch into classical? Decoherence is part of the story but not all.
✕ Falsified — physics's phlogiston pile
Ideas that looked promising and were ruled out. Listed for historical company.
aetherMedium for light. Killed by Michelson-Morley (1887).
caloricHeat as a substance. Replaced by kinetic theory (~1850).
phlogistonSubstance released during combustion. Replaced by oxygen (Lavoisier, 1770s).
steady stateUniverse without a beginning. Killed by the CMB (1965).
hidden varsLocal hidden-variable theories. Killed by Bell tests (1972 onward).
MOND-onlyModified gravity without dark matter. Disfavoured by cluster lensing and CMB peaks.
ultraviolet catastropheClassical blackbody prediction. Killed by Planck (1900).
Bohr atomOrbital electrons in fixed shells. Replaced by quantum mechanics (1925).

Waiting for Mendeleev

What did Mendeleev actually do in 1869? He sorted the known elements by a single property (atomic weight) and noticed periodic regularity. The regularity became a pattern, the pattern became a law, the law became a prediction — germanium, gallium, scandium, all found in the decade after, exactly where the table said they should be. Once the periodic table existed, chemistry stopped being a list of accidents and became a deductive science.

Physics has not yet had its Mendeleev. We have the entities and the puzzles, but no organising principle that turns "why three families?" into "of course, look at the column." The geometric turn in quantum field theory is one candidate for where the cleanup starts. So is the cobordism hypothesis. So is geometric Langlands. So is whatever new symmetry someone notices next year.

Until then, the table above is the honest snapshot — what we know, what we suspect, what we have ruled out, and the questions still floating around the edges. Mostly a mess.
