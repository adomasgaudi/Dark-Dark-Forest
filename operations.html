The old menu sorted drills by material only: natural numbers, integers, reals, vectors. Useful, but a little one-eyed. This page sorts by what you are actually doing: adding, dividing, raising to powers. Pick the operation family first, then drop into the right practice lane.

Why this page exists

Powers of 10 were never the problem. The menu was. Operations cut across multiple number systems, so this page gives them their own front door instead of pretending exponentiation belongs to just one material.
