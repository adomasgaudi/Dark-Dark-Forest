Unit 5a — Quantum mechanics

Grades 10–11 · Quantum
When classical physics breaks
Classical physics predicts that a hot object should radiate infinite energy. It doesn't. Planck's fix was desperate — he assumed energy comes in discrete chunks. Einstein confirmed it with the photoelectric effect. Classical physics is broken. What replaces it?
Physics
  • Blackbody radiation: the ultraviolet catastrophe
  • Photoelectric effect: light as particles
  • De Broglie: matter as waves
  • Schrödinger equation — qualitative
  • Heisenberg uncertainty principle
  • Pauli exclusion from antisymmetry
  • Spinors: the electron needs 720° to return to its original state (optional deep dive)
  • Stern-Gerlach experiment (optional deep dive)
Chemistry
  • Electron orbitals: why those shapes
  • Periodic table as quantum consequence
  • Why noble gases don't bond
  • Nodes as rotations in the complex plane
Math
  • Euler's formula eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ
  • Imaginary numbers as physically real rotations
  • Matrices as operators on state vectors (optional deep dive)

Unit 5b — Relativity

Grade 11 · Relativity
What "fixed relative to what?" did to physics
Maxwell's equations give a fixed speed for light. Fixed relative to what? Einstein took it seriously — the speed of light is the same for everyone, always. This breaks time, space, and mass as we understood them.
Physics
  • The problem with Maxwell + Newtonian mechanics
  • Speed of light: invariant and why this matters
  • Time dilation
  • Length contraction
  • E = mc²: derivation sketch
  • General relativity: gravity as spacetime curvature (optional deep dive)
Math
  • Spacetime interval: the invariant quantity
  • Lorentz transformation
  • 4-vectors (optional deep dive)

Unit 5c — Human biology

Grades 11–12 · Human Biology
Physics builds the body
A protein is a chain of atoms. Its shape determines its function. For 50 years nobody could predict the shape from the sequence alone. AI just solved it — using physics.

  • ATP and the mitochondria
